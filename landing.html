About LENSCRAFT

We are a team of passionate visual storytellers based in New York. With over a decade of experience, we specialise in capturing authentic emotions and turning fleeting moments into timeless art.

Our approach is unobtrusive, artistic, and deeply personal. We believe that every client has a unique story, and we pour our heart into every frame.

Our Vision

To create imagery that resonates beyond the surface — honest, evocative, and full of life.

Our Values

Integrity, creativity, and a relentless pursuit of excellence in every project we undertake.

Our Services

From intimate portraits to large-scale events, we offer a full spectrum of professional photography services tailored to your needs.

Wedding

Elegant, candid coverage of your special day. We capture the laughter, the tears, and every in-between moment.

Portraits

Natural and stunning portraits that reflect your personality — from personal branding to family sessions.

Events

Corporate galas, private parties, and everything in between. We document the energy and essence of your event.

Fashion

Editorial and commercial shoots with a refined aesthetic. We bring your creative vision to life.

Product

High-quality product photography that highlights every detail and elevates your brand.

Nature

Capturing the beauty of the natural world — landscapes, wildlife, and environmental storytelling.
